Key Insights & Recommendations

YouTube Dominance: YouTube accounts for 63.5% of all KOL posts, making it the primary platform for Tribit's influencer marketing. Recommend maintaining strong YouTube partnerships while exploring growth opportunities on other platforms.
Regional Distribution: India leads with 47% of KOL activity, followed by US (24%) and Europe (29%). Consider balancing regional efforts to ensure global brand presence.

Platform Diversification: Instagram and TikTok are underutilized compared to YouTube. Develop platform-specific content strategies to increase engagement on these high-growth platforms.
Cross-Platform Strategy: Some KOLs like Unbox Therapy are active across multiple platforms. Leverage these multi-platform influencers for integrated campaigns.
